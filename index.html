Nicholas (Jing) Yuan

LinkedIn ↗Google Scholar ↗

Dr. Nicholas (Jing) Yuan currently serves as Technical Advisor to the Corporate Vice President (CVP) & Partner at Microsoft, focusing on strategy development, long-range planning, and technical/product innovations. He is also an Adjunct Professor at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology. Nicholas has a proven track record in globally impactful AI research, AI product development, and AI commercialization, combined with extensive experience in leading technological innovation. Previously, he was Vice President of AI, Chief Scientist and Director of the Language & Speech Innovation Lab at Huawei Cloud, where he led a global team to drive the adoption and commercialization of AI and big data technologies across industries such as smart cities, healthcare, manufacturing, finance, and energy. Before that, he served as Deputy General Manager of the AI Creation Business Team and as a Senior Applied Scientist Lead/Manager at Microsoft. During this time, he spearheaded the development of Microsoft’s Xiaoice AI-Generated Content technologies, including groundbreaking products like the “Image-Inspired Poetry” system and a suite of AIGC offerings. Earlier in his career, he worked as a researcher at Microsoft Research. Nicholas holds a Ph.D. in Computer Science and a B.S. in Mathematics, both from the University of Science and Technology of China.

Nicholas has authored more than 100 papers published in or accepted by leading conferences and journals, including KDD, NeurIPS, ACL, AAAI, WWW, ICDE, UbiComp, TKDE, and TOIS. He has regularly served as a program committee member or chair for leading international conferences, as well as a reviewer for top journals in the fields of data mining and AI. His research has been featured multiple times by influential media outlets such as MIT Technology Review. In 2021, he was recognized as one of the Most Influential Scholars in the data mining domain by Aminer AI 2000, ranking No. 12 worldwide and Top 2 in China.

Nicholas has received numerous prestigious honors, including the Microsoft Fellowship (2011), the Best Paper Award at the IEEE International Conference on Data Mining (2013), the Best Student Paper Awards at ACM SIGKDD (2016, 2018) and CVPR (2026). He also received the Best Paper Runner-up Award at ACM SIGSPATIAL (2010) and the Distinguished Doctoral Dissertation Award from the Chinese Academy of Sciences (2013). In 2018, he was awarded the First-class Prize of the Natural Science Award by the Ministry of Education in China. Additionally, he earned the 10-Year Impact Honorable Mention at ACM SIGSPATIAL (2020), the Test-of-Time Award at ACM SIGKDD (2022), and the Test-of-Time Award at IEEE MDM (2023). Nicholas is a Fellow of the IEEE.
