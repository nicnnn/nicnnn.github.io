Dr. Nicholas (Jing) Yuan is a Partner at Microsoft and Technical Advisor to the Corporate Vice President (CVP), focusing on strategy, long-range technology planning, and technical and product innovation. He is also an Adjunct Professor at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology. His work spans AI research, product development, and commercialization, alongside the leadership of multidisciplinary technical teams.

Previously, he served as Vice President of AI, Chief Scientist, and Director of the Language & Speech Innovation Lab at Huawei Cloud, leading a global team to apply AI and big data technologies across cities, healthcare, manufacturing, finance, and energy. Before joining Huawei, he was a Senior Applied Scientist Lead/Manager and Deputy General Manager of the AI Creation Business Team at Microsoft. He led the development and application of Bing’s Asia-Pacific knowledge graph, as well as AI creation technologies and AIGC products for Microsoft Xiaoice. Earlier, he was a researcher at Microsoft Research Asia. He holds a B.S. in Mathematics and a Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Science and Technology of China, where his doctoral advisor was Academician Guoliang Chen.

Nicholas has authored more than 100 papers published in or accepted by conferences and journals including KDD, NeurIPS, ACL, AAAI, WWW, ICDE, UbiComp, TKDE, and TOIS. His research has received Best Paper or Best Student Paper Awards at KDD, ICDM, and CVPR, Test-of-Time Awards at KDD and MDM, and a 10-Year Impact Honorable Mention at SIGSPATIAL. He also received the First-class Prize of the Natural Science Award from China’s Ministry of Education and regularly serves on conference program committees and as a journal reviewer in AI and data mining. Nicholas is a Fellow of the IEEE.
